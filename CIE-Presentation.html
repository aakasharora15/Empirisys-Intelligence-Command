The Challenge

A genuinely superior product
that doesn't sell itself

In the HSE software market, three compounding forces cause winnable deals to stall — not because of the product, but because the advantage is invisible at the moment of decision.

🔒
Incumbent Lock-In

Buyers have years of COMAH-mandated safety data embedded inside Sphera, DNV Synergi Life and Enablon. Switching platforms reads as operational and regulatory risk — not opportunity. Inertia, not merit, governs most renewals. The decision defaults to the incumbent every time.

👁
The Parity Illusion

Every vendor now markets "AI" and "predictive analytics." A domain-trained safety intelligence engine that can surface leading indicators weeks before an incident looks identical to a competitor's marketing veneer to a time-poor HSE buyer. Real differentiation is invisible at the point of decision.

⚠️
The DIY Substitute

Customers' own Copilot and ChatGPT deployments offer a cheap, in-house "good enough" alternative. They appear credible until their reasoning must be defended to a regulator or coroner. Generic tools have no auditability, no safety-domain governance and no repeatable outputs — but they're free.

At exactly the three moments Empirisys is best placed to win — a recent HSE failure, a new Director auditing inherited systems, or board pressure for evidence-based risk management — the opportunity reverts to the incumbent by default. The CIE was built to stop that.

System Architecture — Four Layers
🖥 Presentation Layer
Next.js 16 App Router · React Server + Client Components · Tailwind CSS design system. Premium Dark intelligence-terminal aesthetic — UX engineered to signal analytical gravitas to a technical, sceptical buyer.
14 pages · 37 components
🛡 Application / API Layer
13 server-side route handlers. All credentials are environment-variable–only and never shipped to the browser. Every route validates and type-checks input, applies rate limiting, and scopes queries to the authenticated session.
TypeScript · Zod validation · rate-limited
🧠 Intelligence Layer
Governed Retrieval-Augmented Generation wrapping Anthropic's Claude API. Server-controlled system prompts encode Empirisys doctrine — every answer frames BOOST as a System of Intelligence, every incumbent as a System of Record. Not configurable by the end user.
Claude API · governed RAG · server-side prompts
🗄 Data Layer
PostgreSQL with the pgvector extension. HNSW approximate-nearest-neighbour index for fast semantic search across 142,500 vectors. B-tree indexes for exact filtering by competitor, source type and date. One row per tracked organisation in the competitors table.
PostgreSQL · pgvector · HNSW · 142.5K vectors

Three moments where the CIE changes the outcome

The platform is designed around the three "buy-trigger" moments — the windows where a decisive intervention changes the result from incumbent renewal to BOOST win.
